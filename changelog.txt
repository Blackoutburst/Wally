26/10/2021

**Profile customisation is BACK!!!**

```diff
+ !sfc/!setfontcolor [color code (HEX)] | so you can now change the color of the text on your stats
+ !rfc/!resetfontcolor | put back the text color to white
+ !sf/!setfont (file required format .TTF/.OTF) | change your stats font
+ !rf/!resetfont | reset the text font
+ !setbg/!setbackground (file required format .JPG/.JPEG/.PNG Recommended size 1000x440) | change your stats background
+ !rbg/!resetbackground | remove your background
+ !stats and !pstats display the overall rank

* Light text shadow on every canvas
* Improved sub thread stability
* Practice stats and practice compare stats were not in the same order
* Stats canvas are now the same size of the compare one (for custom background compatibility)
```
(Note if you had a background in the previous version of Wally they might still exist)

Any unreadable color / font or background will be removed, abuse will be sanctioned and NSFW / NSFL background will result in a ban
